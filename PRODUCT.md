# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Peers, collaborators, and community members in the tech and AI space — engineers, researchers, academics, and builders who discover Angelo through GitHub, social, talks, or the blog. The primary action is connection: reaching out to collaborate, exchange ideas, or follow his work. Secondary audiences include prospective clients evaluating him for freelance work and academic contacts from his lecturing roles.

## Product Purpose

Angelo Ortiz Vega's personal portfolio. It establishes professional presence and invites collaboration by showing who he is, what he builds, and how to reach him. Success means a visitor leaves with a clear sense of his engineering profile and a reason to get in touch.

## Positioning

Senior-level, English-fluent Fullstack AI Engineer and Tech Lead based in Costa Rica — a specific and scarce profile in Latin America. Angelo owns the full arc from architecture and ML integration to the user-facing product: he ships AI-powered features at scale, drives technical decisions across a cross-functional team of 20+, and brings ML from research to production in real logistics systems. That combination of end-to-end ownership, AI depth, and production track record in a startup context cannot be truthfully copied by a neighboring developer portfolio.

## Operating Context

- Discovered via GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, or direct links from blog posts and talks.
- Viewed on desktop and mobile; audience is technically fluent and reads code-adjacent content easily.
- Available in English and Spanish; language switching must remain a first-class feature.
- Hero greeting cycles through 15+ languages as a deliberate personality signal, not decoration.
- The site is self-hosted and continuously updated as projects, roles, and content evolve.

## Product Principles

1. **Specificity over generality.** Every claim should be grounded in a real role, project, or outcome — not category-level assertions about "passion for technology."
2. **The work is the brand.** The portfolio earns credibility through what it shows, not how it styles itself. Craft serves the content, not the reverse.
3. **Bilingual is identity, not translation.** EN/ES parity reflects Angelo's operating reality and should be treated as a first-class feature, not an afterthought.
4. **Community over conversion.** The primary mode is Experience + Persuade — invite connection, not transact a hire. The tone is peer-to-peer, not applicant-to-employer.
5. **Costa Rica is a differentiator, not a disclaimer.** Location is named early and proudly; it signals a scarce profile, not a geographic constraint.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Custom accessibility modal ships with the site, providing cursor adjustment, zoom, grayscale, and invert controls. Bilingual support (EN/ES) is a confirmed requirement.

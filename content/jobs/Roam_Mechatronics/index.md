Research and Development engineer focused on automotive-system design for four-wheel off-road vehicles and development of electric two- and three-wheelers.

- Designed and developed core subsystems for the Roam Air electric motorcycle, including battery development, battery connector design, 72 V powertrain architecture, and electronics packaging.
- Designed and developed the Griffin programme: plug-and-play electric conversion kits for Land Cruisers and Land Rover Defenders. Converted 30 vehicles before the company pivoted to ground-up electric-vehicle development to capture greater market share.

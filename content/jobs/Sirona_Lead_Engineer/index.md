- Took over the commercial-scale Direct Air Capture plant programme, rated at 30,000 tonnes of CO₂ removal per year with a containerised modular architecture, at the Design Verification Test gate. Drove the plant through Production Verification Test to a tested, deployment-ready state at handover.
- Designed the plant-wide instrumentation network, including instrumentation cabinetry and harness architecture across the complete Direct Air Capture stack. Designed and built eight automation cabinets for the commercial plant.

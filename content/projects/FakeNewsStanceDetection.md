CS 229 project at Stanford. Extracted n-gram, sentiment, polarity and cosine-similarity features from 75,385 news articles, then compared multinomial Naive Bayes, SVM, softmax and a multi-layer neural network to predict whether a headline agrees with, disagrees with, discusses, or is unrelated to its body.

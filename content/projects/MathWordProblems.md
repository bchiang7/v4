CS 224N final project at Stanford. Preprocessed the AQUA-RAT dataset and extracted mathematical equations from multiple-choice explanations with regular expressions, then implemented a Transformer network and a bidirectional GRU-LSTM with attention to generate equations that solve algebraic word problems.
